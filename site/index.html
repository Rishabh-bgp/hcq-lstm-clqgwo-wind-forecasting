道 How it works

Each LSTM gate gets an extra term from its own 2-qubit variational quantum circuit, trained with a physics-informed loss. The full repo can also tune hyperparameters with the CLQGWO grey wolf optimizer. The model predicts the 10-minute change in wind speed and adds it to the current reading.

Weights were trained in PyTorch, exported to JSON and re-implemented in plain JavaScript, including a small statevector simulator for the quantum circuit. A test checks that the JS and Python predictions match within 1e-4.
